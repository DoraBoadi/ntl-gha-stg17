    Who lives without detectable light?

    9,819,138 people — 29.8% of the inhabited population — live in cells that are inhabited but emit no detectable night-time light, against 13,704,193 in 2015. The largest concentrations are listed in the table; they are the natural starting point for a targeted off-grid or grid-extension programme. Depending on the noise floor (0.15–0.5 nW·cm⁻²·sr⁻¹), this figure ranges from 9,839,059 to 10,010,561: that is the indicator's real uncertainty.

    How did the lit footprint change?

    The lit footprint moved from 19,061 to 56,016 km² (+193.9%), with 37,261 km² newly lit and 305 km² losing detectable light. The sprawl ratio of 0.54 indicates a pattern of densification rather than outward spread.

    Is light concentrating or spreading?

    Population-weighted inequality in light per capita across ADM2 units narrowed from 0.471 to 0.405 (Gini). 21% of total inequality sits between ADM1 regions and 79% within them. Initially dimmer units grew faster, indicating convergence.

    Which districts changed significantly?

    Median ADM2 growth in Sum of Lights is +19.2% per year. Growth is measured between the median of the first and last three years. 1 unit(s) exceed the robust +2.5σ threshold and 0 fall below −2.5σ; these are candidates for investigation, not confirmed findings — each should be checked against the gas-flare list, the product version and any known local event. 26 unit(s) with a very low initial base are shown but never flagged. Neighbouring districts grow alike (Moran's I = 0.26): flagged changes often cluster and should be read as regional rather than local phenomena.

    Any month-level anomaly?

    Monthly Sum of Lights shows a seasonal amplitude of 1.65× between the brightest and dimmest calendar month — a reminder that month-on-month comparisons are meaningless without seasonal adjustment. 24 observation(s) deviate by more than 2.5 robust sigma from their own month-of-year baseline.

    Limitations — read before citing

    • Thresholds: noise floor 0.25, urban core 10.0 nW·cm⁻²·sr⁻¹; every 'unlit' figure depends on the first of these.
    • The brightest 1 % of pixels hold 49.0% of the national Sum of Lights.
    • Gas-flare candidates detected: 2. Verify against petroleum infrastructure before interpreting.
    • Night-time lights do not measure electricity access, GDP or population — every such reading is an inference.
    • Activity that emits no light is invisible here: subsistence agriculture, daytime informal trade, indoor activity.
    • No DMSP-OLS year is compared with a VIIRS year; the two are different physical quantities.
